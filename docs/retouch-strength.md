# Turning retouch up or down after Auto retouch

After **Auto retouch** has run on a photo, the Retouch workspace shows **Retouch strength**: one
slider for each kind of retouch it did on that photo - blemish and acne removal, skin smoothing,
even skin tone, dark circles and eye bags, teeth whitening, shine removal, body skin, clothing,
background cleanup and so on. Only the kinds it actually did are shown.

- **100%** is exactly what Auto retouch did. **0%** takes that kind of retouch away completely.
- Release the slider and the photo re-renders. Every change is an ordinary step in History and
  can be undone.
- Some sliders go above 100%. A slider stops where every area in it would already be at full
  strength, so it never pretends to do more than it can; one that is already at full strength
  stops at 100% and shows **max**.
- **Reset to Auto retouch** puts every slider back to 100%.
- **Apply these strengths to the collection** gives every other photo that Auto retouch has
  already retouched the same strengths. Each photo keeps its own measured retouch - 70% blemish
  removal takes away 70% of the marks found on *that* photo. Photos Auto retouch has not run on
  yet are left alone.

Running Auto retouch again keeps your sliders where they are.

Changes you make by hand are not affected: an operation you add, or an automatic one you open and
change, has its own strength control in **Saved retouch operations**, and the sliders leave it
alone.
