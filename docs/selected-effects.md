# Effects you place yourself

Open **Retouch**, choose a tool from the **Tool** list, place it with the ellipse, the brush, a
gradient or an AI selection, and click **Apply retouch**. Every effect is a saved operation:
it can be selected again, changed, turned off, reordered, undone, and given its own strength.

## Liquify

- **Push** - moves what is inside the ellipse in the direction you set. Tuck in a waistline, lift a
  jaw, smooth a stray hairline.
- **Enlarge** - for eyes, curls, flowers. **Shrink** - for a nose or a waist. **Twirl** - rotates.

Every warp fades to nothing at the edge of the ellipse, so it never leaves a seam. Use small
ellipses and low strength: a little goes a long way. Nothing is ever reshaped automatically.

## Blur and optics

- **Spin blur** around a point you pick. **Blur** and **Pixelate** for hiding a face, a number
  plate or a screen.
- **Soft focus** (a diffusion filter) and **Orton glow** for a dreamy, glowing look.
- **Sharpen** for eyes, jewellery, lace and hair, without sharpening the skin next to them.
- **Noise reduction** removes colour speckle and grain while keeping edges sharp.
- Purple and green fringes on branches and window frames are removed by **Defringe** under
  Cleanup, which measures the lens's colour fringing from the photograph.

## Colour

- **Hue shift** turns colours around the colour wheel and keeps their brightness.
- **Green screen** - pick the screen colour on the photo and choose what replaces it; the green
  light spilled onto the subject is removed. Choose **Only remove the spill** to keep the screen.
- **Unify colour** evens patchy hair colour, faded clothing or uneven paint toward its own average.
- **Local white balance** - pick something that should be white or grey; just that area is
  corrected. For mixed window and tungsten light.
- **Reduce visible veins** on hands, feet and legs. **Invert** makes a negative.

## Light and effects

**Light leak**, **Glitch**, **Haze** (use a gradient for fog that thickens with distance), **Sun
rays** from a point you pick, and **Film halation**, the red glow film shows around bright lights.

## Portrait and scene

- **Add catchlight** - draw a small ellipse on the iris where the reflection of the light belongs.
- **Pet eye / eye glow fix** darkens glowing pupils of any colour; a dark pupil is left alone. Keep the selection on the pupil: a brightly lit, strongly coloured iris can be darkened too.
- **Lip gloss** lifts the highlights the lips already have.
- **Add freckles** - choose density and size; moving the area gives a different pattern.
- **Floor reflection** - select the floor; what stands above it is mirrored into it, fading down.
- **Drop shadow** - select the subject; a soft shadow falls beside it in the direction you set.

## Fix and clean up

- **Dust & scratches** removes sensor dust, specks and fine scratches; edges wider than the radius
  stay sharp. Raise the threshold if it touches texture you want.
- **JPEG artifact cleanup** smooths the small square blocks of a heavily compressed photo that
  came from a phone or a messaging app. Real edges are kept.
- **Remove banding** smooths the visible steps in a sky or a plain backdrop; textured areas are
  left alone.
- **Auto levels** and **Remove colour cast** fix just the area you select - a dull dress, a
  hazy window, a green cast from foliage on white fabric.
- **Even out uneven lighting** flattens a backdrop lit from one side, keeping its detail.
- **HDR detail** brings out large-scale structure - clouds, buildings, fabric folds - and can
  open the shadows.

## Creative looks

**Bokeh highlights**, **Tilt-shift**, **Duotone**, **Posterize**, **Halftone print**, **Pencil
sketch**, **Oil paint**, **Cross-process**, **Infrared**, **Mirror symmetry**, **Lens flare** and
**God rays** from a light you pick, and **Snow & rain**. Each works only inside the area you
select, and each is a saved step you can change or undo.

## Colour looks

**Split tone** (two colours for shadows and highlights), **Film fade** (milky blacks),
**Bleach bypass** (gritty, desaturated contrast), **Teal & orange** (skin keeps its own colour),
**Colour splash** (pick one colour to keep; the rest of the area turns black and white) and
**Restore blue sky** (clouds stay white, skin is left alone).

## Light, lens and art

**Spotlight**, **Rim light** (select the subject; its edge catches light from the side you choose),
**Chromatic aberration**, **Fisheye**, **Anamorphic streaks**, **Kaleidoscope**, **Watercolour**,
**Stained glass**, **Cartoon**, **Neon edges** and **Thermal camera**.

## More repairs

**Repair blown highlights** gives a yellow forehead or a cyan sky the colour of the area around it,
keeping its brightness. **Dehaze (selection)** clears haze from just the area you select.
**Limbal ring** - draw the ellipse around an iris; its outer ring is darkened for clearer eyes.

## Shortcuts

Under **Shortcuts** in each effect: enlarge eyes, add hair volume, slim a nose, lift a jawline,
hide a face or plate, dreamy glow, sharpen the eyes. Each one chooses one of the tools above and
sets it up; adjust it afterwards like any other.

None of these tools finds the area on its own, and none of them creates new picture content: they
work with the pixels already in the photograph.
