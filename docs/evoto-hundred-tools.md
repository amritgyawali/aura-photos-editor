# A hundred portrait, attire, background and delivery tools

These tools work the way Evoto's do: you move one slider and AURA finds what it acts on - every face,
the lips, the hair, the clothes, the background - and measures it on your photograph. They are AURA's
own; Evoto's software was not taken apart to make them, so they will not match Evoto pixel for pixel.

Nothing here runs by itself. Every tool is one step in your history, so Undo takes it back.

## Where they are

- **Develop > Portrait studio** - the tabs **Face**, **Body**, **Skin**, **Makeup & colour**,
  **Hair & attire**, **People & scene**, **Background** and **Faces**.
- **Develop > Framing, delivery and review** - ID photographs, crops, cut-outs and social exports,
  and the check for faces showing no white of the eye.
- **Effects** (sidebar) and **Retouch** - the seven tools you place yourself.

## Face (Face tab)

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Eye height | Raises or lowers the eyes. |
| Eye tilt | Lifts or drops the outer corners of both eyes. |
| Inner corners | Opens the inner corners toward the nose. |
| Outer corners | Lengthens the outer corners. |
| Eye symmetry | Measures how open each eye is and opens the smaller one toward the larger. Two eyes of a size are left alone. |
| Nose bridge, nose tip, nose lift, nostrils | Each part of the nose on its own. |
| Cheek apples, temples | Fuller apples of the cheeks; filled-out temples. |
| Upper lip, lower lip, mouth position | Each lip on its own; the mouth raised to shorten the space under the nose. |
| Brow height, brow arch, brow thickness | The eyebrows' position, the lift of their tails, their thickness. |
| Lower face length, chin width | The lower face and the chin. |
| Hair volume | More volume at the crown. |

## Body (Body tab)

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Thighs, calves | Slims each. |
| Flat stomach | Pushes in the side of the stomach that bulges past the line from the ribs to the hips. |
| Hip lift, shoulder line | Lifts the hips; squares or relaxes the shoulders. |
| Torso length, height | Lengthens the torso, or the whole figure below the shoulders. |
| Neck slim | Slims the neck. |
| Double chin | Lifts a double chin and evens the shadow of its fold. |
| Level head | Turns a tilted head toward level, measured from the eyes. |

Shape tools bend what is around them a little at strong settings, as every reshaping tool does.

## Skin (Skin tab)

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Aegyo-sal (eye smile) | A soft lift along each lower lid with a faint shade under it. |
| Lip lines | Smooths fine vertical lines across the lips, keeping their shape and shine. |
| Dewy glow | A glow on the forehead, nose, cheekbones and chin, where light already falls. |
| Stubble shadow | Evens the blue-grey shadow of shaved stubble toward that person's own cheeks. A beard is kept. |
| Rosy complexion | Turns sallow skin a few degrees toward rose. Brightness is not changed. |
| Even teeth | Brings a darker or yellower tooth toward the others, never whiter than the brightest. |
| Sharpen features | Sharpens eyes, lashes, brows and lips; never the skin. |
| Beard definition | Deepens and defines a beard or moustache. |
| Clothing colour on skin | Takes out the colour a red sari or a green wall throws onto skin, measured from the clothing beside it. |
| Refine pores | Reduces pores on the nose and cheeks; finer grain and anything deeper than a pore are kept. |

## Makeup (Makeup & colour tab)

Eyeliner (drawn on the lid line measured from each eye), eyeliner wing, fuller lashes, lower lashes, lip
liner (in a deeper shade of the lips' own colour), ombre lips, highlighter, bronzer, **enhance makeup**
(lipstick, eyeshadow and blush already worn, made richer against the person's own cheek colour) and
**fill brows** (gaps filled with the colour of the brow's own darkest hairs).

## Tools you place yourself (Effects and Retouch)

Soften body hair, smooth cellulite, even tan lines, rejuvenate hands, even dark knuckles and elbows,
remove a glasses coating's reflection colour, and remove a cast shadow. Each works only inside the
area you draw and measures that area: the tan line is the boundary between its two levels of skin,
the shadow is relit to the lit surface around it.

## Framing, delivery and review

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| Passport 35x45 mm, US 2x2 in, Visa 33x48 mm | Measures the head from crown to chin, crops to the standard head size, levels the eyes and puts the person on white. Says so when a photograph is too tight for the standard. |
| Headshot, half body, full body, square, story | Measured crops around the main face or the whole person. |
| Level the eyes | Straightens the photograph so the eye line is level, cropping just enough to leave no empty corners. |
| Transparent PNG cut-out | The people on a transparent background. |
| Instagram grid | Three squares a row, numbered in the order to post them. |
| Carousel slides | A wide photograph as 4:5 slides that swipe seamlessly. |
| Before and after | The original and your edit side by side, in the same framing. |
| Enlarge 2x | Twice the size with a Lanczos filter that does not leave halos at edges. |
| Faces (Portrait studio) | Choose which faces the face, skin and makeup tools apply to. |
| Find faces showing no white of the eye | Lists the photographs in a collection with a face whose eyes show no white. A list to look through, not a blink verdict: closed eyes land on it, and so do squints, glare on glasses and faces under a veil, while a closed lid with pale shimmer can be missed. |

Exports are written only into the folder you choose, and never over a file that is already there.

## What these tools cannot do

They rest on AURA's own face finder and region measurements. A face it does not find - often a small
face at the back of a group - gets no face or skin tool; draw it in Portrait retouch first. Expressions,
eyelids, teeth shape and generated backgrounds need trained generative models AURA does not include,
and telling a blink from a squint needs a trained eye-state model it does not include either.
