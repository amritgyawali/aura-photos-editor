# Additional editing methods

Open a photograph in Photo Studio. The **Colour lab**, **Spatial repair**, and
**Masks → Selection tools → Selection laboratory** panels now offer 75 methods
each. Create a mask first, choose a method and its controls, then apply it. Colour
methods can be searched by name or description. Stacks support up to 32 ordered
operations; strength zero preserves the photo. Undo restores the saved edit.

The **Delivery statistics**, **Surface review**, and **Lighting geometry** screens
add 25 measurements to the existing 45. Run photo inspection explicitly after
saving an edit. Moving between these screens retains the report; changing the
saved photo revision invalidates it. Charts show their units and sample support.
Fine detail is measured on a small preview and should also be reviewed at 100%.

The [capability ledger](reports/AURA-capabilities-2026-10-22.json) lists each new
method and its mechanism. Creative tone/colour operations can intentionally
produce strong results: choose a suitable mask, start at low strength, and compare
before and after. These are independent AURA methods; exact Evoto equivalence and
trained-model parity have not been established.
