# ADR-0138: Native local detail and advanced editing methods

Status: accepted

The previous qualification passed functional routes while documenting reduced
spatial resolution, full-frame colour scratch and incomplete Evoto parity. A
functional save is insufficient evidence of native-detail preservation or of
another application's numerical output.

Add 25 distinct colour, 25 spatial and 25 selection mechanisms, plus 25 read-only
diagnostics. Keep original implementation provenance and explicit resource
limits. Use bounded native-resolution tiles for supported local spatial methods,
immutable selected source support and complete halos at tile boundaries. Retain
the reduced grid for global/geometric operators. Avoid the retained RGB target
plane for local colour stencils. Validate active selection controls and bound
source-dependent work before preview loading. Correct lagged-correlation support
means and version the renderer cache.

Qualification combines numerical fixtures, the complete native/frontend suites,
all four tracked capability ledgers in the rebuilt desktop, original-bound photo
proxies, and verified RAW exports. Preserve prior attempts and failed evidence.
Record functional passes separately from Evoto parity. Inspection remains a
bounded descriptive preview; neither diagnostic counts nor file-inventory
coverage are model reconstruction or perfection measurements.
