Open an AURA delivery

Choose the folder AURA exported into. Each photograph opens with:

  • the rendered pixels as the base layer;
  • AURA's regions as layer masks;
  • AURA's retouch as a separate layer you can switch off.

AURA's exposure, colour temperature, tone curve and HSL settings do not come across. They act in a linear working space Photoshop has no equivalent of, and a translation would look close and drift the moment you moved a slider. Change them in AURA and re-export.
